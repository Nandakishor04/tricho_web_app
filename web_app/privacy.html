🔬 About Tricholens

Tricholens is an AI-powered trichoscopy diagnostic application designed to assist clinicians and patients in the early detection and monitoring of scalp and hair disorders, with a primary focus on Androgenetic Alopecia (AGA).

Androgenetic Alopecia is the most prevalent form of hair loss, affecting both men and women worldwide. Early and accurate diagnosis is critical to slowing progression and tailoring effective treatment strategies.

How Tricholens Works:
Using your device camera or photo library, Tricholens captures high-resolution scalp images and analyses them through a calibrated deep-learning model. The system evaluates:

  • Hair follicle density (hairs/cm²)
  • Miniaturised hair ratio (%)
  • Scalp condition classification (AGA, Oily Scalp, Healthy)
  • Vellus-to-terminal hair distribution

📊 Information We Collect

We collect basic personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and location when you create an account.

We also collect health-related inputs such as scalp images, patient history, treatment records, and diagnostic results to provide personalised clinical services.

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide AI-powered scalp and hair health assessments
  • Allow clinicians to review diagnostic reports and images
  • Manage patient records, treatment history, and follow-ups
  • Improve model accuracy and overall application performance

🛡️ Hair Images & Medical Data

Scalp images and diagnostic results you submit are processed securely within the application and are shared only with registered clinicians for professional review.

Tricholens does not sell, distribute, or publicly disclose your personal, medical, or biometric data to any third party. All data handling is conducted in accordance with applicable data protection regulations.
