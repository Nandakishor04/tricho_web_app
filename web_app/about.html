🎯 About Tricholens

Your personal hair health companion

🚀 Our Mission

Tricholens helps people understand, track, and improve their hair health using technology and expert medical guidance.

📋 What We Do

  • Provide hair health quizzes and assessments
  • Allow users to track hair growth with photos
  • Connect users with qualified doctors
  • Offer professional feedback and prescriptions

💖 Why Tricholens?

Hair concerns can affect confidence and well-being. Our goal is to make professional hair care guidance accessible, convenient, and personalized.

🩺 Medical Collaboration

We work with licensed doctors who review reports and hair images to provide safe and professional recommendations.
