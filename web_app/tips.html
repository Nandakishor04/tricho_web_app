Hair Tips

✨ Daily Hair Tips

Expert advice for healthier, stronger hair

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drinking enough water keeps your hair roots hydrated and prevents breakage.

🔥 Avoid Excessive Heat

Minimize use of hair dryers and straighteners. Heat damages hair protein structure.

🥚 Eat Protein-Rich Food

Hair is made of protein (keratin). Include eggs, nuts, and fish in your diet.

💆 Massage Your Scalp

Regular scalp massages improve blood circulation, stimulating hair growth.

🛏️ Use Satin Pillowcases

Satin reduces friction against the hair cuticle, preventing frizz and breakage.

✂️ Trim Regularly

Getting a trim every 8-12 weeks helps remove split ends and maintain hair strength.
